What every denial told you

A shared access signature is not a password. It is a signed statement of exactly what may be done, to what, from where, and between which two moments. Every one of those is a separate decision, and the failure mode is always the same: a token that works, issued wider than the job needed. Assignment 4.2 asks you to document what your token permits, what it forbids, and what happens after it expires, with evidence for both states.
